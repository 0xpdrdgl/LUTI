## Design Context

### Users
Two audiences with similar weight, both deciding whether to hire LUTI Arquitetura (Luiza Tinoco):
- **Residential clients** (families/individuals) commissioning a house or apartment project — an emotional, visual, trust-driven decision. They browse the portfolio looking to feel "this understands how I want to live."
- **Commercial/investor clients** (businesses, incorporadoras) commissioning commercial spaces — a more evaluative decision, looking for proof of technical competence and a coherent body of work.
Site currently serves visitors in Manaus-AM (in-person) and nationwide Brazil (remote).

### Brand Personality
**Editorial, atemporal, sofisticado** (editorial, timeless, sophisticated) — confirmed by the client's own copy ("abordagem editorial", "sofisticação atemporal", "rigor técnico e sensibilidade humana"). The site should read like a design magazine feature on the studio's work, not a sales brochure or a SaaS product page.

### Aesthetic Direction
Current implementation: serif display font (Playfair Display) for headings, Inter for body/labels, light neutral palette (#fafafa bg / #0c0c0c ink / #3d3d3d body / #6e6e6e muted / #d6d6d6 accent hairlines), generous whitespace, uppercase tracked labels, thin 1px borders instead of cards/shadows, large full-bleed hero photography. This direction is sound and matches the stated personality — the critique should protect and sharpen it, not replace it.

(High-end Brazilian architecture-studio sites — editorial photography-led layouts, restrained typography, minimal chrome. Use as the calibration bar for "premium enough.")

No anti-references given — evaluate against generic AI-portfolio-template tells instead (identical card grids, generic icons, gradient accents, centered everything).

### Design Principles
1. **Photography leads, chrome follows** — the work (projects) is the product; typography and layout should frame it, never compete with it.
2. **Editorial restraint over decoration** — no cards-with-shadows, no icon-above-heading pattern, no gradients; hairline rules and generous whitespace do the separating.
3. **One serif moment per view** — Playfair Display marks the emotional/important line (a section's main heading); everything supporting stays in Inter, uppercase-tracked for labels.
4. **Content must earn its section** — every section on the home page should say something the others don't; redundant claims (e.g. two "we work nationwide" notes) break the editorial trust this brand relies on.
5. **Dual-audience clarity** — residential and commercial visitors should each find proof relevant to them without extra scrolling/hunting; don't let one audience's content crowd out the other's.
